## 🧩 Comprehensive Feature Breakdown

### 🎯 Core Mechanics Module
This module serves as the **central nervous system** of the suite. It integrates seamlessly with the game's native UI to provide:

- **Dynamic Cast Adjustment**: Fine-tune throw distance, lure depth, and retrieval speed through an intuitive slider interface.
- **Weather Override Console**: Take manual control of in-game meteorological conditions. Sunny skies for surface feeders, overcast for bottom dwellers—you decide the forecast.
- **Bait & Lure Customizer**: Modify the attraction radius of specific lures. Increase their visual appeal to nearby species or transform them into ultra-specialized tools for a single target.

### 🏆 Legendary Hunter's Toolkit
Focused exclusively on the **pinnacle of achievement**—catch rates for rare, unique, and event-specific fish:

- **Spawn Point Highlighting**: Visual markers that outline the precise coordinates of legendary fish spawn areas. Updated dynamically with game patches.
- **Conditional Trigger Alerts**: Set custom notifications based on specific times of day, weather patterns, or bait usage that historically correlate with legendary catches.
- **Trophy Value Calculator**: Before you even reel in, view the estimated trophy score based on environmental variables and current gear loadout.

### ⚡ Flow Acceleration Module
Dedicated to removing **friction from repetitive actions**:

- **Macro Action Recorder**: Record a sequence of button presses, rod movements, and camera angles. Replay these sequences flawlessly to perform complex maneuvers without manual input.
- **Auto-Loot & Inventory Sorting**: Automatically categorize new catches, dispose of common items, and optimize your storage capacity based on pre-set rules.
- **Travel Waypoint System**: Mark popular fishing hotspots on the map. The suite calculates the **optimal navigation route** between your selected waypoints.

### 🛡️ Resilience & Protection Suite
Focused on **longevity and sustainability**:

- **Durability Monitor**: Real-time tracking of rod, line, and reel integrity. The suite proactively alerts you before potential breakage during critical fights.
- **Reactive Recovery Cooldown**: Reduce the waiting period between failed casts or lost lures. Shortens downtime, allowing for **continuous engagement**.
- **Adaptive Difficulty Tuning**: Adjust the AI aggression of fish. Make them more predictable for a relaxation session, or crank up their resistance for a challenging bout.

### 🌐 Global Connectivity Layer
Enhances the **social and competitive experience**:

- **Leaderboard Analyzer**: Scrape and interpret global rankings to identify top-performing gear setups and popular fishing zones.
- **Community Challenge Tracker**: Sync with seasonal in-game events. Receive a checklist of tasks and track completion progress in a dedicated sidebar.
- **Multi-Language Dictionary**: Full translation support for the suite's UI, covering all major languages including English, Japanese, Spanish, German, and Portuguese.
